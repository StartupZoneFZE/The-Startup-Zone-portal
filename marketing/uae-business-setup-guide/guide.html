Both let you own 100% of your company. The right choice depends on who your customers are and where you'll do business.

Our view for overseas investors: if you serve international clients, sell online or offer consulting, a free zone is usually faster, simpler and more cost-effective. You can always add a mainland branch later as you grow.

DAY05

Decide owners and structure

Today's goal

Agree who owns the company, in what shares, and who manages it.

Action 1

Set up ownership

  1. One owner or several partners? This decides your company type.
  2. Agree share percentages in writing before you apply.
  3. Choose the manager/director and who will sign for the bank.
Action 2

Individual or company as shareholder?

  1. Individual shareholder: passport-based and the simplest option.
  2. Corporate shareholder (your company abroad owns it): needs parent company documents.
  3. Parent company documents must usually be notarised and attested for the UAE. Allow extra weeks.
Startup Zone pro tip
If speed matters, start with yourself as an individual shareholder. Company names like FZE, FZCO or FZ-LLC vary by free zone. We'll explain which one fits you.

DAY08

Prepare your document pack

Today's goal

Have clear, correct scans of every document ready to submit.

Action 1

Scan your documents

  1. Passport: colour scan, all corners visible, 6+ months validity.
  2. Passport-size photo with a white background.
  3. Proof of home address (utility bill or bank statement, last 3 months).
  4. UAE visa or entry stamp page, if you have one.
Action 2

Check the quality

  1. Save as clear PDF or JPG. Not cropped, no glare.
  2. Name files clearly, e.g. Passport_JohnSmith.pdf.
  3. Check your name is spelled the same on every document.
Startup Zone pro tip
Blurry scans are the number one cause of delays. Use a phone scanner app in daylight instead of a quick photo. It takes two minutes and saves days.
Documents needed
  • Passport
  • Photo
  • Proof of address
  • CV (for some activities)

DAY16

Understand your visa journey

Today's goal

Know which visa you need and the five steps to get it.

Action 1

Know the visa types

  1. Investor / partner visa: for shareholders of the company.
  2. Employment visa: for staff your company hires.
  3. Residence visas are typically valid for 2 years and renewable.
Action 2

Learn the five steps

  1. Entry permit (issued before you travel).
  2. Enter the UAE, or change status if already here.
  3. Medical fitness test.
  4. Emirates ID biometrics.
  5. Residence visa issued, linked to your Emirates ID.
Startup Zone pro tip
You must be in the UAE for the medical test and biometrics. Plan a trip of about a week, and we'll book your appointments so every day counts.

Register for Corporate Tax

Today's goal

Register your company for Corporate Tax and get your tax number.

Action 1

Know the rules

  1. Every UAE company, including free zone companies, must register.
  2. 9% Corporate Tax on taxable profit above AED 375,000; 0% up to that.

DAY27

Decide on VAT

Today's goal

Find out whether you must register for VAT, or should choose to.

Action 1

Check the thresholds

  1. Mandatory: taxable sales over AED 375,000 in the last 12 months, or expected in the next 30 days.
  2. Voluntary: sales or expenses over AED 187,500.
  3. The standard VAT rate is 5%.
Action 2

If you register

  1. Register on EmaraTax and receive your Tax Registration Number (TRN).
  2. Show your TRN on every tax invoice.
  3. File VAT returns (usually quarterly) within 28 days of each period end.
Startup Zone pro tip
Track your sales every month from day one. Registering late after crossing the threshold leads to penalties. We'll tell you exactly when you need to register.

FAQ
FAQ

Questions overseas investors ask us

Do I need to be in the UAE to set up my company?

No. Your license can be issued fully remotely. You only need to visit for your visa medical test and Emirates ID, and often for your bank meeting.

Can I own 100% of my company?

Yes. Free zone companies are 100% foreign-owned, and so are most mainland activities.

How long does it take?

A free zone license is often ready within a week. With your visa, plan for about 3–4 weeks. Bank accounts vary and can take 2–6 weeks.

Do I have to deposit share capital?

In most free zones you don't need to deposit it upfront, but rules vary. We'll confirm for your free zone.

Can I keep living abroad?

Yes, but to keep your residence visa you should generally not stay outside the UAE for more than 6 months in a row.

Will I pay tax?

There's no personal income tax. Your company may pay 9% Corporate Tax on profit above AED 375,000, or 0% on qualifying free zone income. VAT is 5% once registered.
