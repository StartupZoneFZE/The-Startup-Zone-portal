# Startup Zone audit: 10 changes most likely to make money

Audit date: 14 Nov 2026. All work was read-only. Step-by-step record: `AUDIT_LOG.md`.

Ranking is by size of the money at stake and how quickly it can be reached. Figures marked "my estimate" are simple arithmetic on your data, not forecasts.

## 1. Contact every lead within minutes, and follow up on a fixed schedule

- 5,673 of 7,703 lost deals in Zoho CRM carry the loss reason "Missed Follow Ups". Another 438 are "Lack of response".
- 55 leads created since 15 Jul 2026 are still "Not Contacted". 43 of them arrived in one batch on 22 Sep.
- A website form lead from 7 Nov was still "Not Contacted" on 14 Nov.
- "Contacted Date" is filled on 0 leads. 14 calls have been logged since 1 Aug.
- Meta spend in 2026 is about AED 35K for roughly 1,340 form leads and 2,440 WhatsApp conversations.

Change: one owner per lead, first contact inside 5 minutes in working hours, then a set sequence (for example day 0, 1, 3, 7, 14) by WhatsApp and call, enforced by CRM tasks. Re-contact the "Missed Follow Ups" list with one WhatsApp message.

## 2. Send the quotes that are sitting in draft

- Latest 200 estimates in Zoho Books (2 Oct to 13 Nov 2026): 125 became invoices (AED 646,131). 75 are still drafts (AED 657,465).
- 60 of the drafts are more than 7 days old (AED 552,645). 38 are more than 14 days old.
- No estimate is marked sent, accepted or declined, so nobody can see which quotes are waiting on the client.

Change: send or void every draft this week; mark estimates as sent; chase each one at day 2 and day 5.

## 3. Run renewals as a campaign, starting 90 days before expiry

- 110 client licences expire between 14 Nov 2026 and 14 Feb 2027. None is marked "Renewal In Progress". "Agreed Renewal Price" is empty on all 110.
- 96 clients are sitting as Expired, and 55 of the licences that lapsed in the last 12 months ended as Cancelled.
- Invoices containing a renewal item total AED 1.47M across 154 invoices in 2026, about AED 9,500 per invoice (my estimate). At that average, the 110 upcoming renewals are about AED 1M of billing.

Change: quote a renewal price to each of the 110 now; offer instalments; call the 96 expired clients with a reinstatement offer.

## 4. Sell a paid monthly accounting and tax plan to existing clients

- 406 clients have an active licence. About 200 are corporate tax registered; 43 are marked "Filing By Client"; return status is tracked on 1.
- VAT returns are filed by you for 3 clients.
- "Bookkeeping" items appear on 6 invoices in 2026 (AED 11,451). None of 105 invoices checked is a recurring invoice.
- Your blog quotes an "All-Inclusive Monthly Plan: AED 450 a month + VAT". It is not on the pricing page or the bookkeeping page.
- Competitors publish plans: Creative Zone AED 750/month, A&A Associate AED 999 / 2,199 / 3,599, BCL AED 400 to 1,200 (search summaries, unverified).
- 100 clients at AED 450/month is AED 540K a year (my estimate).

Change: put the AED 450 plan on the pricing page, set it up as a recurring invoice, and offer it to every active client before their corporate tax deadline.

## 5. One price list, with a minimum fee

- The same service is billed at different prices: corporate tax filing assistance AED 450 / 550 / 750; CT registration 250 vs 500; ANCFZ 1-visa package 7,500 / 7,999 / 8,150 / 10,900 against a list price of 10,800; service fee 1,000 / 4,000 / 5,000.
- In a 45-invoice sample the explicit fee line had a median of AED 976 and appeared on 24 invoices.
- The Books catalogue has 622 items; 301 have no rate; 44 are duplicates.

Change: fix a rate for each service, set a floor below which a discount needs your approval, and clean the catalogue.

## 6. Chase overdue invoices automatically

- 93 invoices are overdue for AED 275,422 across 79 customers.
- Reminders sent on those 93 invoices: 0. Viewed by client: 0.
- AED 62,681 is under a year old (AED 48,425 under 180 days). AED 212,741 is more than a year old.

Change: switch on Zoho Books payment reminders (before due, +3, +7, +14 days), add a payment link to each invoice, and review the year-old balances one by one.

## 7. Fix the website pages that cannot produce a lead

- Only 1 lead with source "Website Form" exists in the CRM for 2026.
- The VAT, bookkeeping, bank account, freezone and mainland pages have no form and no WhatsApp or quote button.
- The VAT page shows "At [Company Name], we offer a range of VAT services".
- The cost calculator page contains "Preview: form not connected yet." and leaves out SHAMS.
- Mainland is priced three ways: "Ask us", AED 14,999 and "from AED 11,000". Old figures remain in the pricing cards' code.
- The Testimonials page has no testimonials. The Terms link is broken on every page. The address shown is Dubai.

Change: add a WhatsApp button and short form to each service page, fix the placeholder, calculator and prices, and fill the testimonials page from your 200+ Google reviews.

## 8. Record where each sale came from

- Lead Source is blank on every 2026 deal. UTM and ad name fields are empty on leads.
- Closed Won deals in the CRM are mostly test records, so wins are not recorded there.
- WhatsApp campaigns cost AED 5 to 8 per conversation; form campaigns AED 11 to 25 per lead. Nothing shows which of them produce paying clients.
- No Meta Pixel was found in the page code (it may be inside Tag Manager, which could not be checked).

Change: pass the ad and campaign into the CRM lead, mark deals won when invoiced, and move budget each month to the campaigns that produce invoices.

## 9. Publish a priced page for each free zone and each tax service

- 224 of 406 active licences are ANCFZ. There is no page for SPC, Meydan has one old post, and corporate tax, golden visa, PRO and visas have no service page.
- No price is shown for VAT, bookkeeping or corporate tax.
- Avyanco and A&A Associate have a dated cost page per free zone; four of your five named competitors publish accounting or tax prices (search summaries, unverified).
- 23 new guides published 3 to 13 Nov are missing from the sitemap.

Change: one page per free zone with the year-one and renewal price in the title, one page per tax service with a price, and add the new guides to the sitemap.

## 10. Advertise instalments and a referral reward

- RAKEZ offers Tabby instalments on setup and renewal and a 3-year price guarantee. Creative Zone offers a referral reward up to AED 3,500 (search summaries, unverified).
- Your Books catalogue already has Tabby and Tamara payment-link items, so instalments are offered but not advertised on the site.
- You have 406 active clients and 200+ Google reviews, and no referral offer on the site.

Change: state "pay in instalments" on the pricing page and in ads, and give existing clients a fixed reward for each referred licence.

## Not money, but fix now

- The live Supabase database password and service-role key are committed in `SUPABASE_CREDENTIALS.md` and `PROJECT_STATUS.md` in this repository. Rotate both.
- A Zoho Flow webhook address with its key is visible in the website's page source, so anyone can inject fake leads.

## What was not audited

- Client email content and reply times: mail moved to Zoho Mail on 27 Oct 2026 and no connector exists in this session.
- Zoho Desk tickets: the connection lacks permission to read them.
- WhatsApp conversations in Respond.io: not authorised.
- Competitor sites could not be opened from this environment; their prices come from search-result summaries.
- The invoice sample is 45 invoices (about 10% of value), weighted to large invoices.
- Website forms were not submitted, so delivery of form leads into the CRM is untested.
